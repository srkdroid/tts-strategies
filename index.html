❓ TTS Strategy Calendar — User Guide

📅 1. Interactive Deployment Calendar View

View scheduled option strategy deployments across calendar months mapped to exact trading days.

  • Holiday Awareness: Automatically adjusts for Indian F&O market holidays and expiries.
  • Shifted Expiry Notice: Displays an orange badge (Holiday Adjusted) when a deployment date moves to the previous trading day.
  • How to Use: Click 📅 Calendar in the top filter bar and click any strategy card to open full details.

📊 2. Capital Flow & Timeline (Gantt Chart View)

Visual horizontal timeline showing entry dates, active durations, and exit points across calendar months.

  • 🟣 Monthly Strategies: Long DTE monthly strategies (e.g. Batman 1:3:2, No Brainer).
  • 🔵 Bi-Weekly & Weekly Strategies: Medium/short DTE strategies (e.g. Auto-Pilot, Double Decker).
  • 🟢 STBT Strategies: Short Term Buy Today / Sell Tomorrow overnight strategies.
  • How to Use: Click 📊 Capital Flow in the filter bar. Navigate months using ◀ Prev Month / Next Month ▶.

🔍 3. Multi-Criteria Filter Bar

Filter deployments across the entire application instantly without page reloads.

  • Index: Filter by Nifty, Bank Nifty, Fin Nifty, Midcap Nifty.
  • Strategy Type: Filter by PE & CE, Directional PE, Directional CE, Hedged Spread.
  • My Portfolio: Toggle to view only your bookmarked strategies.

🧮 4. Interactive Strategy Modal & ATM Strike Calculator

Inspect setup legs, target profit, stop loss, and calculate exact strike prices live.

  • Live ATM Calculator: Type any custom ATM price into the ATM: box to calculate exact strike numbers for all PE and CE legs.
  • Reset Button (↺): Reset ATM price back to default benchmark value.
  • How to Use: Click any strategy card or timeline row to open the modal.

⭐ 5. Starred Portfolio System ("My Portfolio")

Save your frequently traded strategies to a custom portfolio for quick access.

  • How to Use: Click ⭐ Add to Portfolio inside any strategy modal or row. Check ⭐ MY PORTFOLIO in the filter bar anytime to view only your saved strategies.

📝 6. Knowledge Base & Strategy Notes System

Read operational guidelines, margin rules, and adjustment protocols formatted in Markdown with GitHub callouts.

  • Drag-to-Resize Sidebar: Click and drag the vertical divider bar between the sidebar and reader pane to adjust width dynamically.
  • Categorized Notes: Filter notes across Generic, Strategy, Discretionary, and Adjustment guidelines.
  • How to Use: Click 📝 Notes in the top header bar.

📖 7. Strategy Master Catalog (Cards & Single-Line List View)

Browse all strategies including 0-Adjustment, Discretionary, and Adjustment repair strategies.

  • Dual Layout Views: Toggle between 🎴 Card View (rich cards) and 📋 Single Line List (compact single-line summaries).
  • Category Filters: Filter by ⚡ 0 Adjustment, 🎯 Discretionary, or 🛠️ Adjustments.
  • Calendar Isolation: Discretionary and Adjustment strategies are on-demand only and stay isolated from calendar deployment timelines.
  • How to Use: Click 📖 Strategy Catalog in the top header bar.

📔 8. Interactive Trade Journal & Multi-Level RoI Analytics

Log live trade executions, track margin, record entry/exit/psychology notes, and analyze multi-level RoI performance.

  • Auto-Populate Legs: Selecting any strategy auto-populates setup legs, with freedom to add/remove legs, adjust quantities, strikes, and entry prices.
  • Margin & Exit Tracking: Capture Margin Used (₹) on entry. Record leg exit prices, brokerage (₹), and STT/taxes (₹) on exit to compute Net Realized P&L & Trade RoI %.
  • 3-Way Journaling: Record Entry Rationale Notes, Exit Review Notes, and Mindset & Psychology Notes.
  • Integrated Multi-Level Analytics: Switch to the 📈 Performance & RoI Analytics sub-tab inside Trade Journal to view:
    • 📊 Category Level RoI Breakdown: Monthly, Bi-Weekly/Weekly, STBT, Discretionary & Adjustments.
    • 🎯 Strategy Level Performance: Win Rate %, Margin Used, Net P&L, and Realized RoI % per strategy.
    • 🗓️ Timeframe Breakdown: Aggregated performance across Daily, Weekly, Monthly, Quarterly, & Annual timeframes.
  • How to Use: Click 📔 Trade Journal in the top header bar.

💰 9. Capital Allocation Planner & Probability-Weighted RoI Engine

Simulate 100% week-by-week capital utilization, win rates, and expected net returns across your portfolio.

  • Capital Base Simulation: Enter your custom total capital (e.g. ₹ 50,00,000) to simulate exact rupee allocation across all weekly deployment cycles.
  • Week-wise Heatmap Matrix: Track capital deployment percentages across W1–W5 cycles to eliminate idle capital leakage.
  • Probability-Weighted Expectancy: Adjust win rate percentages per strategy bucket to calculate expected net monthly returns and annualized RoI %.
  • How to Use: Click 💰 Capital Planner in the top header bar next to Trade Journal.

☀️ 10. Theme Toggle & 🚪 Secure Logout

  • Theme Toggle: Click ☀️ / 🌙 in the header bar to switch between dark and light modes.
  • Logout: Click 🚪 Logout to end your session safely anytime.
